About MI TECH

Made for the work behind the buying desk.

MI TECH presents Business Management System—a practical way for businesses to manage the people, deliveries and records behind buying agricultural and raw products.

Built around the work your team already does

When deliveries come in, there are weights to record, supplier details to check and people who need the right information. Business Management System brings those tasks together—from individual and bulk receiving to supplier histories, receipts and business reports.

It is made for businesses that buy and handle agricultural and raw products: cashew, cocoa, shea nuts and more. It supports the buying and receiving operation, not growing crops. Admins oversee the business, while Secretaries have tools for their day-to-day work.
