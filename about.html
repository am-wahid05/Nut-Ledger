About Nut Ledger

Digitizing agricultural commodity operations in Ghana.

Nut Ledger is a custom data management platform built for agricultural commodity logistics—connecting what happens at the warehouse scale to the decisions made in management.

The challenge

Nut Ledger was engineered for an agricultural commodity logistics firm in Ghana facing a critical supply-chain problem: fragmented paperwork, manual data entry and delayed financial reporting.

Field workers and administrative staff relied on manual records to track farmer deliveries, commodity weights and aggregator activity. Every handwritten entry and every re-typed figure created opportunities for errors, missing records and reporting delays.

The solution: a dual-portal system

Nut Ledger connects operational data entry with executive oversight. When Secretaries record commodity weights and aggregator metrics at the warehouse—through individual or bulk receiving—the platform processes and synchronizes that data in real time.

That data powers a centralized Admin dashboard with automated analytics, supplier histories and structured financial statements, so management no longer waits on paperwork to understand the business.

The impact

  • Accuracy: weights and deliveries are captured once, at the source.
  • Accountability: role-based access and connected records show who recorded what.
  • Discrepancy detection: analytics help management spot inconsistencies early.
  • Faster reporting: structured financial reports are generated from recorded data.

The result is a more transparent, data-driven workflow that connects warehouse operations to management decisions.

Who it's for

Nut Ledger is made for businesses that buy and handle agricultural and raw products—cashew, cocoa, shea nuts and more. It supports the buying, receiving and reporting operation, not growing crops.
