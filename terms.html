Terms for using the service.

This draft explains the basic product and subscription terms. It is not ready to govern a production service until Nut Ledger confirms the open policy details and obtains qualified legal review.

Draft for legal review—not legal advice. Confirm applicable Ghanaian law, subscription and seasonal-pause policy, account restrictions, support and availability commitments, payment and cancellation procedures, and liability terms with qualified counsel before publication.

1. The service

Nut Ledger is a business-management application for organizing supplier information, individual and bulk receiving, business records, user roles, receipts, reports and related business functions. Features may change as the service develops.

2. Accounts and access

Customers are responsible for providing accurate account information, maintaining the confidentiality of their sign-in credentials, using authorized accounts and promptly addressing suspected unauthorized access. Admins manage the business and its users; Secretary accounts have a separate, more operational role and do not receive the same permissions.

3. Trial and subscriptions

The service provides a 30-day free trial. After the trial, the Business plan is GH₵250 per month. The bundle for three secretaries is GH₵75 per month. Subscription prices are stated in Ghana cedis (GH₵). Do not assume additional plans or billing terms beyond those confirmed by Nut Ledger.

4. Subscription due dates and grace period

A grace period applies when a subscription becomes due. If an Admin subscription becomes restricted after the applicable grace period, the Admin application may become locked or restricted. The Secretary application continues operating and synchronizing according to the product's subscription rules. Nut Ledger should communicate applicable timing and account rules to the customer.

5. Seasonal subscription pauses

Seasonal businesses may be eligible to pause a subscription during an off-season only as allowed by the actual subscription policy. The pause process, eligibility, notice period, billing effect, duration and effect on access must be confirmed by Nut Ledger and communicated to the customer before a pause is agreed. No pause, discount or special pricing is promised by these draft terms.

6. SMS receipts and credits

SMS receipts use separate credits at GH₵0.05 per SMS. SMS delivery depends on the configured SMS provider and telecommunications networks. The customer is responsible for using appropriate recipient contact information and content and for obtaining any permissions required for messages it sends.

7. Acceptable use

Customers and users must use the service lawfully and responsibly. They must not attempt to gain unauthorized access, interfere with service operation, misuse another person's data, send unlawful messages or use the service to violate another person's rights.

8. Customer business data

The customer retains its rights in business data it provides, including supplier records and receiving information. The customer is responsible for its authority to enter that data and for the accuracy and lawfulness of its use. The customer permits Nut Ledger to process that data only as needed to provide, secure and support the service and as otherwise agreed or required by law.

9. Nut Ledger intellectual property

Nut Ledger and its licensors retain their rights in the software, design, brand, documentation and related intellectual property. These terms grant the customer permission to use the service for its business while its account is authorized; they do not transfer ownership of the software.

10. Synchronization, local storage and backups

The application uses cloud synchronization and local storage, including SQLite, for local functionality. Synchronization requires an internet connection. Customers should verify that records have synchronized and review available backup options rather than treating a local copy as a guaranteed cloud backup. Nut Ledger should provide details of backup and restoration practices separately.

11. Service availability and maintenance

The service may be unavailable temporarily for maintenance, updates, connectivity problems or events outside reasonable control. No uptime percentage or uninterrupted availability is promised by these draft terms. Nut Ledger should provide support and maintenance contact details and notice practices before publication.

12. Business Assistant and analytics

The Business Assistant helps users understand or navigate information related to their business. AI output may be incomplete or inaccurate and must be checked against business records and professional sources. Analytics and reports are views of recorded business information; they are not financial, accounting, legal, tax or investment advice.

The Business Assistant provides assistance based on available business information. It is not a substitute for professional financial, accounting, legal, tax, or investment advice.

13. Third-party services

The service may depend on third parties for cloud, authentication, SMS and other technical functionality. Those providers may have their own terms and availability limitations. Nut Ledger should identify material providers and relevant customer-facing terms before production use.

14. Restrictions and termination

Access may be restricted for subscription reasons described in these terms, suspected misuse, security concerns or legal requirements. Nut Ledger should give notice and an opportunity to address a problem when appropriate and lawful. The customer may ask Nut Ledger how to close an account and request its data; closing an account does not transfer ownership of Nut Ledger software.

15. Data on subscription expiry

Business data is not deleted simply because a subscription expires. Access may be limited as described above. Customers should contact Nut Ledger with questions about continued access, export or deletion and review the Privacy Policy for data-request details.

16. Warranties and liability

The service is a business tool and does not replace customer judgment, verification of records or professional advice. Any warranties, limits of liability and remedies must be stated in final terms reviewed for applicable law; no broader exclusion or cap is created by this draft.
