How it works

A clear path from delivery to business insight.

Set up your business and team, record receiving, keep supplier history connected, then review the activity in your records and analytics.

Request a Demo →
01

Create your business

Set up company details, products and available company settings. Multiple businesses are supported, with business data separated by company.

02

Set up users and roles

You manage the business workspace and users as an Admin. Your Secretaries have a separate set of tools for their day-to-day work, with access that does not include every Admin function.

03

Record what comes in

Record each bag's weight on its own, or use bulk receiving to enter a bag count and total weight. Either way, your deliveries stay together as clear, searchable records.

04

Keep supplier records close

Search for a supplier, open their profile and pick up the story from there: recorded deliveries, history and statements.

05

Send or print receipts

Print selected saved records or send an SMS receipt using SMS credits at GH₵0.05 per message. SMS credits are separate.

06

See how the business is moving

Use the dashboards, reports and analytics to look back at receiving, products, suppliers and recorded activity. They help you understand your records—they aren't professional financial advice.

07

Stay in step, wherever you work

Admin and Secretary devices sync through the internet, so your team doesn't need to share a Wi-Fi network or work in the same location.
