Privacy Policy

How information is handled.

This draft describes the types of information that may be used by Nut Ledger and this website. It requires final review and completion by Nut Ledger and a qualified legal professional before publication.

Draft for legal review—not legal advice. Nut Ledger should verify its actual data flows, hosting locations, retention periods, third-party services and user-request procedures, and obtain qualified legal review for applicable Ghanaian requirements, before this policy is published or relied on.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy concerns information handled through Nut Ledger (the “service”) and the Nut Ledger marketing website (the “website”). It distinguishes information a business customer enters into the service from information someone provides through this website.

2. Information that may be handled

  • Account information: sign-in identifiers, account and role information, and information needed to administer accounts.
  • Company information: business names, settings, branding and business configuration.
  • Supplier information: supplier names, contact details, identifiers and supplier-profile information entered by a customer.
  • Business records: receiving, product, weight, supplier-history, statement, report and other operational information entered or created in the service.
  • SMS-related information: recipient information, receipt content, sender settings, delivery status and credit or usage information, depending on actual product and SMS-provider records.
  • Technical and local information: data stored locally by the application to support local functionality, including its use of SQLite, and information associated with synchronization and account access.
  • Website information: if the demo form is connected and enabled, it collects the name, business name, email, phone/WhatsApp, business type, team size, message and submission time that you provide. The form sends these details to Nut Ledger's Supabase project to store and handle the request. A notification email is sent to Nut Ledger through its configured email provider. A temporary hash of the network address may be used for rate limiting; the form does not store the raw address as part of the request.

3. Why information is used

Information may be used to provide and operate the service; authenticate users; apply account roles and company separation; record receiving and supplier history; synchronize business information; prepare reports, statements and receipts; provide customer support; maintain backups; protect the service; respond to demo requests; and meet applicable legal obligations.

Information entered into the Business Assistant may be processed to help users understand or navigate their own business information. AI-generated output can be incomplete or inaccurate and is not professional advice.

4. Business customer data

Business data entered by a customer—including supplier information and receiving records—belongs to and is controlled by that customer, subject to the customer's rights and obligations to the individuals concerned. The customer is responsible for having an appropriate basis to enter and use supplier, worker and other personal information. Nut Ledger uses such information to provide and support the service and should not use it for unrelated purposes.

5. Storage, synchronization and service providers

The application uses local storage and SQLite for local functionality and Supabase for backend cloud services and synchronization. Cloud synchronization requires an internet connection. Local data and cloud data may be handled differently depending on the feature and device.

The service may rely on third parties to provide cloud hosting, authentication, SMS delivery, website hosting or other technical services. When the demo form integration is enabled, Supabase stores the request and Resend (or the configured email provider) processes the notification email. The email includes the details submitted in the form so Nut Ledger can follow up. The SMS provider and hosting locations should be confirmed by Nut Ledger before publication. Third parties should receive only information needed to provide their service and should be governed by appropriate arrangements.

6. SMS receipts

When a customer chooses to send SMS receipts, recipient contact information and message content are used to prepare and deliver those messages through the configured SMS service. SMS messages cost GH₵0.05 each and use separate SMS credits. SMS delivery may depend on the relevant provider and mobile network.

7. Security

Access to the service uses accounts and role-based functionality. Business data is separated by company, and database access controls form part of the application architecture. Users should protect account credentials and devices. No method of transmission or storage can be represented as risk-free; no security certification is claimed here.

8. Cookies, local storage and analytics

The marketing website does not currently use cookies, browser local storage, analytics or marketing tracking services. This statement should be updated if analytics, third-party embeds or other tracking are introduced. See the Cookie Policy.

9. Sharing and disclosure

Information may be shared with service providers needed to operate the service, at the customer's direction (for example, sending a receipt), or when disclosure is required by applicable law. This draft does not make a claim about any other sale or disclosure practice; Nut Ledger must confirm and accurately describe its actual practices before publication. Exact service providers and any relevant transfer arrangements also require confirmation.

10. Retention and subscription expiry

Demo requests are retained so Nut Ledger can respond and maintain appropriate business records. A final retention period and any email-provider retention should be confirmed by Nut Ledger before publication. Business data is not deleted simply because a subscription expires. An Admin application may become restricted after the applicable grace period while the Secretary application continues operating and synchronizing according to subscription rules. Specific business-data and backup retention and deletion timelines have not been supplied and must be confirmed before this policy is finalized.

11. Your requests and rights

Account holders may contact Nut Ledger to ask about access, correction, export, restriction or deletion of information, or to raise a privacy concern. Requests may require verification and coordination with the relevant business account because a business may control records it entered. Nut Ledger should respond in accordance with applicable requirements and explain any relevant limits.

12. Data deletion requests

Contact amwahid005@gmail.com to ask how to request deletion of account or business information and to confirm the applicable account-owner steps, any information that must be retained, and how backups or legal obligations affect the request. Do not assume that deleting an application from a device deletes cloud records.

13. Children

The service is intended for business operations, not for collecting children's information. If information about a child is found in a business record, contact Nut Ledger so it can be reviewed.

14. Changes and contact

Nut Ledger may update this policy as the product or its data practices change. The revised version should identify its effective date once approved. For privacy questions, contact amwahid005@gmail.com.
