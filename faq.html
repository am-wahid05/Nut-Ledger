FAQ

Good questions make for a better start.

Learn what Business Management System does, how the roles work and what to expect from receiving, synchronization and pricing.

What is Business Management System?

It is a business-management platform for managing suppliers, recording individual and bulk receiving, organizing business records and reviewing operations through dashboards and reports.

Who is it designed for?

It is designed for agricultural and raw-product buying businesses, aggregators and small-to-medium businesses that buy, receive, record, manage and analyze products—not for managing crop cultivation.

Can it manage cashew, cocoa and shea nuts?

Yes. These are examples of agricultural and raw products. The product is not limited to these product types.

What is the difference between Admin and Secretary?

The Admin manages the business, users, settings and administrative tools. Secretary functions focus on day-to-day operations such as receiving, today's records, printing and permitted SMS tasks. Secretary permissions are not the same as Admin permissions.

Can I record multiple bags at once?

Yes. Bulk receiving records a number of bags and a combined weight. Individual receiving supports entering weights for individual bags.

Can I manage supplier history?

Supplier profiles can be searched and linked to receiving history. Supplier statements and supplier records are also available.

Can I send SMS receipts?

Yes. SMS receipts use SMS credits and cost GH₵0.05 per SMS. SMS is not free.

Can I print receipts?

Yes. Saved receiving records can be selected for printing.

Do Admin and Secretary need to be on the same Wi-Fi?

No. Devices synchronize through the cloud and internet and can be in different locations.

How does synchronization work?

The product synchronizes through the cloud using an internet connection. It also uses local storage and SQLite for local functionality.

Is my business data separated from other companies?

The product supports multiple businesses and separates business data by company.

How much does it cost?

There is a 30-day free trial. After the trial, Business Management System is GH₵250/month. The Secretary bundle for three secretaries is GH₵75/month. SMS is GH₵0.05 per message, using separate credits. See the Pricing page.

What happens after the 30-day trial?

The monthly Business plan price is GH₵250 after the trial. Secretary bundle and SMS charges are listed separately. Subscription grace-period rules apply when a subscription becomes due.

What happens if my subscription expires?

A grace period applies when a subscription becomes due. If an Admin subscription becomes restricted after the applicable grace period, the Admin application may be locked or restricted. The Secretary application continues operating and synchronizing according to product subscription rules. Business data is not deleted simply because a subscription expires.

Can I pause during the off-season?

Seasonal subscription pauses are subject to the applicable subscription policy. Contact MI TECH to confirm the current off-season process and conditions.

Does the system work with Excel?

Administrative tools include Excel import/export and a spreadsheet workspace. Ask for a demo to confirm the file formats and workflows you need.

Can I request a demo?

Yes. Use the Request a Demo form. The form currently shows a local success message but is not connected to a submission service; until one is configured, contact MI TECH directly.

What does the Business Assistant do?

It helps users understand and navigate information related to their own business. It is not a substitute for professional financial, accounting, legal, tax or investment advice.
