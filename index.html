Visit all FAQs →
Do Admin and Secretary need to be on the same Wi-Fi?

No. Devices synchronize through the cloud and internet, even when used from different locations.

Can I record multiple bags at once?

Yes. Bulk receiving records a number of bags and their combined weight. Individual receiving supports bag-by-bag weights.

Will my business data be deleted if a subscription expires?

No. Business data is not deleted simply because a subscription expires. Admin access may become restricted after the applicable grace period.
