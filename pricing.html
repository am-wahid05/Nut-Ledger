Subscription grace period

A grace period applies when a subscription becomes due. If the Admin subscription is restricted after the applicable grace period, the Admin application may become locked or restricted. The Secretary application continues operating and synchronizing according to the product's subscription rules.

Your data is not deleted for expiry alone

Business data is not deleted simply because a subscription expires. Expiration may affect application access as described above.

Seasonal operations

Seasonal subscription pauses during off-season periods are governed by the applicable subscription policy. Ask Nut Ledger to confirm the current pause process and conditions for your business.

SMS credits

SMS receipts consume credits at GH₵0.05 per message. SMS is not included as free usage in the stated plan price.

No annual plans, discounts, promotional prices or payment methods are advertised here because they have not been provided.
